What goes in it

    The weeks after

      Tuesday reminders

      Save a reminder for every Tuesday at 1:00 pm for the next year, telling you which bin goes out. Your phone’s calendar then does the reminding.

      This saves a file called mybin-reminders.ics. Tap it when it has downloaded and choose your calendar app to add the reminders.

      Based on the 6-week pattern starting Wednesday 07/10/2026. Collections can move around Christmas, New Year and bank holidays – please check the council’s notices at those times.
